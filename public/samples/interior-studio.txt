WHAT WE DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Residential interiors -- Full-home design for apartments, villas and
heritage tharavads: layouts, storage, finishes, colour and lighting.

Turnkey renovation -- Knock-through work, plumbing and electrical
rewiring, joinery, painting and handover with a snag list closed.

Bespoke joinery -- Kitchen units, wardrobes, TV cabinets and puja units, library
walls and beds, cut and assembled by our own team, never sub-contracted.

Lighting design -- Lux studies, circuit planning and fixture selection so
you do not end up with six downlights and one dark corner.

Sourcing & restoration -- Antique teak and rosewood pieces recovered from
old houses in Alappuzha and Thrissur, repaired and re-finished.

Commercial & hospitality -- Boutique stays, cafes, clinic and studio fit-outs
where the brief is durability plus a photographable corner.

Design-only consultation -- Drawings, 3D views and a specification list for
the client's own contractor. Two revisions included.

THREE JOBS, WITH NUMBERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1) Sea-facing apartment, Panampilly Nagar, 1,850 sq ft, Mar-Nov 2025
   Two-bedroom flat with salt damage and a kitchen that could not breathe.
   We rebuilt the kitchen in BWP plywood with marine hardware, replaced the
   balcony glazing, and moved the sleeping wing away from the road.
   Outcome: 19 weeks from demolition to handover against a 26-week Kochi
   benchmark; final billing within 3.1 percent of the approved estimate;
   connected lighting load cut from 1,460 W to 1,005 W, a 31 percent drop
   the client verified on her Kerala State Electricity Board bill.

2) Eight-room heritage stay, Fort Kochi, completed January 2026
   A 1930s Dutch-style bungalow converted to guest rooms without losing the
   original flooring or the nadukavu. We designed 62 kg of teak furniture in
   house and reused 22 salvaged roof tiles and nine door shutters.
   Outcome: the property listed on booking platforms 11 days after handover;
   owner-reported RevPAR rose 22 percent year on year in its first quarter;
   zero joinery callbacks in the twelve-month warranty window.

3) Home office and library, Kakkanam, 640 sq ft, June 2024 to Aug 2024
   A client working across two time zones needed acoustics, not aesthetics.
   Added a stud-and-wool partition, a 3.2 m wall of load-bearing shelves,
   and layered task lighting at 4000 K.
   Outcome: measured call-back reverberation down from 0.61 s to 0.28 s; the
   client logged 84 fewer minutes of daily background-noise distraction on
   his own tracker; 240 kg of printed paper replaced by 640 shelf-organised
   volumes within a month.

HOW WE WORK
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1 -- Site visit and brief. Free within Kochi corporation limits. We
photograph, measure and ask about your habits, not your Pinterest board.

Step 2 -- Concept and layout, two options, with a rough budget band in rupees.

Step 3 -- Detailed drawings, material schedule and 3D views of three rooms.

Step 4 -- Fixed quote, payment schedule, and a dated work plan you can hold
us to. Nothing is ordered before you sign.

Step 5 -- Build. One site supervisor, a weekly WhatsApp photo log, and a
shared sheet for every change order.

Step 6 -- Snag list, touch-up, handover folder with warranties and a 12-month
joinery guarantee.
