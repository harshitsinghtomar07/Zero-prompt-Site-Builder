SandboxConf 2026

One day in Pune about software that keeps working when the network does not. Local-first data, SQLite at the edge, conflict-free replication & the ugly postmortems behind both.

Saturday 19 September 2026 · 08:30–18:00 · Symbiosis International, Lavale · counting down

About

SandboxConf started in 2024 as six people around a table in a Baner co-working space, arguing about whether CRDTs were worth the complexity. The 2026 edition is a single-track, 320-seat day: no keynote theatre, no vendor demos dressed up as talks, and every session recorded and published within eleven days.

We are deliberately narrow. If your work touches an embedded database, offline sync, WASM modules, or the operational mess of shipping user data that cannot be lost, this is your room. If you want cloud-native Kubernetes generic content, we happily refund your ticket.

Frequently asked

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and it is enforced. Two warnings at the 2024 event, one removal. Reported to the organiser desk or by SMS to +91 98226 11004.

Will talks be recorded?

All stage sessions, published under CC BY-SA within eleven days. The workshop is not recorded because people ask questions with their faces.

Can I expense this to my employer?

Professional tickets carry a GST invoice with our CIN. Write to billing@sandboxconf.dev with your GSTIN before 5 September and we will re-issue.

I have a disability — is the venue accessible?

Ramp access at the Academic Block 3 entrance, two accessible washrooms on the ground floor, and reserved wheelchair rows. Say so on the form and our access lead, Nithya, will call you within two working days — she doesn’t take no for an answer about lift timings.

Refunds?

Full refund till 31 August, 50 percent till 12 September, transferable to another person at any point, no questions.
